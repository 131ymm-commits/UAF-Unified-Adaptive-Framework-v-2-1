In UAF, the Big Bang is not an explosion in space.

$$L_{-1} \to L_0$$

**Testable implication:** the CMB power spectrum encodes the prior of L0. Anomalies in CMB topology may trace the structure of $P_{L_{-1}}$.

## 3. Black holes as divergent external free energy

A black hole is a region where the free energy available to an external observer diverges:

$$\mathrm{BH} = \left\{ x : \lim_{r \to r_s(x)^+} \mathcal{F}_{\mathrm{external}}(r) = +\infty \right\}$$

No external prediction system can model the interior because the prediction error at the horizon becomes infinite — all information becomes inaccessible.

The Bekenstein-Hawking entropy:

$$S_{\mathrm{BH}} = \frac{A}{4\ell_P^2}$$

is interpreted as the maximum number of distinguishable prediction states encodable on the boundary $B_k$ of the black hole system.

Scrambling time:

$$t_{\mathrm{scr}} \sim \frac{\beta}{2\pi} \ln S_{\mathrm{BH}}$$

This is the time for an in-falling state to become fully entangled with the horizon — i.e., the prediction error to be distributed across all boundary degrees of freedom.

## 4. Dark sector as missing free energy terms

Dark matter and dark energy are interpreted not as guaranteed new substances, but as possible missing terms in the free-energy description of cosmological prediction:

$$\mathcal{F}_{\mathrm{cosmo}} = \mathcal{F}_{\mathrm{visible}} + \underbrace{\mathcal{F}_{\mathrm{dark matter}}}_{\text{coherence term?}} + \underbrace{\mathcal{F}_{\mathrm{dark energy}}}_{\text{horizon term?}}$$

**Dark matter** may correspond to a coherence penalty between visible and hidden levels of the cosmological hierarchy — degrees of freedom that contribute to gravitational free energy but are invisible to electromagnetic prediction channels.

**Dark energy** may correspond to the topological term $\mathcal{T}_k$ at cosmological scales — the cost of maintaining the prediction horizon as the universe expands.

This is explicitly speculative. It is marked Layer 4 and requires formal derivation before it can be tested.

## 5. Baryon asymmetry as structural necessity

The matter-antimatter asymmetry ($\eta \approx 6 \times 10^{-10}$) is interpreted as a free-energy gradient during the L−1 → L0 transition.

$$\Delta E \cdot \Delta t \geq \frac{\hbar}{2}$$
