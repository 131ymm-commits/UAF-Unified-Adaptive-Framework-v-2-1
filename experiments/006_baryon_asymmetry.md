# Experiment 006 — Baryon Asymmetry as Structural Necessity
**Layer:** L−1 → L0
**Epistemic status:** Layer 4
**Date:** 2025-07-13 / revised 2026-06-03

## Problem
The universe contains ~10⁹ photons per baryon (η ≈ 6×10⁻¹⁰). Why is there more matter than antimatter? Sakharov conditions (CP violation, baryon number violation, non-equilibrium) are necessary but do not explain the specific value of η.

## UAF Answer
The matter-antimatter asymmetry is a structural necessity of the L−1 → L0 transition, not an accident.

A symmetric universe (equal matter and antimatter) would annihilate to radiation — a state of maximum entropy with minimum prediction structure. The free energy landscape during the phase transition selects against this:

F(symmetric) → ∞ at L1 (no stable prediction hierarchy can form)
F(asymmetric, η > 0) has a stable minimum

The Sakharov conditions are the structural requirements for the L0 prior to develop a persistent prediction hierarchy at L1. CP violation is not a fine-tuned parameter; it is a requirement for the prediction manifold to have a non-trivial topology that admits stable particle hierarchies.

The specific value η ≈ 6×10⁻¹⁰ remains unexplained — UAF reduces "why any asymmetry" to a structural argument but does not yet predict the magnitude.
