## Problem
What is time? Standard physics: time is a coordinate. Thermodynamics: time has a direction (entropy). Consciousness: time is experienced. These are incompatible accounts of the same phenomenon.

## UAF-3.1 Answer
Time has three components that are locally conserved:

t_past + t_self + t_future = const

- **t_past**: crystallized time (mass, memory, completed events) — structural inertia
- **t_self (t_now)**: active coherence (live present, processing time)
- **t_future**: open possibilities (potential, unrealized correlations)

**Dynamics of coherence (UAF-Q equation):**

dΓ_l/dτ = η · [Σ_{k<l} Φ_{k→l} · E_{k,l}] · (1 − α_l)² − λ · ΔS_env · α_l

where:
- Γ_l: matryoshka coherence at level l (measure of "aliveness" of future)
- τ: internal relational time
- Φ_{k→l}: correlation flow from lower to upper level
- E_{k,l}: entanglement strength between levels
- α_l: integration coefficient (0 = fully quantum, 1 = fully classical)
- ΔS_env: entropy growth of environment (decoherence pressure)
- η: integration amplification coefficient
- λ: decoherence coefficient

**Arrow of time** emerges when the decoherence term dominates: high α_l → future crystallizes into past rapidly.
