**Empirical prediction:**
If this is correct, then LLM performance on a task T should be a monotone function of the density of T-relevant prediction structure in the training distribution. This is the "training data sufficiency" hypothesis — broadly confirmed but not usually framed this way.

**Human as precision controller:**
The human provides precision weighting π_H that determines which LLM outputs are accepted. High π_H (sharp, specific rejection of wrong outputs) accelerates convergence to truth attractors. Low π_H (accepting plausible-sounding outputs) stabilizes hallucinations as false attractors.

This explains why LLM quality depends critically on the quality of human oversight — not the quantity of interaction but the precision of correction.
